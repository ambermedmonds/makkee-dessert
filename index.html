More about Makkee
What is it, exactly?
A bowl of dessert you eat with a spoon. Most of ours are sweet soups: a silky base of coconut milk, fresh milk, or house-made mango purée, filled with fruit and toppings.
Is it really sweet?
Less than you'd expect. We keep the sweetness subtle, so the mango, taro, and coconut really come through.
What are the chewy bits?
That's the fun part. Taro balls and mochi are soft and chewy, sago pearls are tiny and bouncy, and grass jelly is silky and lightly herbal.
Is it served hot or cold?
Both. Many of our bowls are served chilled, and we also make nourishing warm sweet soups. Anything marked "Cold only" on the menu always comes cold.
What should I order first?
Our three best sellers are the Coconut Cassava Adazi, Cassava Deluxe, and Taro Black Sticky Rice Mochi Fresh Milk. Love mango? Try the Mango Pomelo Sago.
