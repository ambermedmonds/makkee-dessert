From Huzhou
to Main Street.

Known in China as 麦记牛奶 (Mai Ji Milk), Makkee Dessert gives traditional Chinese sweet soups a fresh, modern twist. Our shop in Downtown Bellevue is the brand's first in Washington.

How We Got Here.

Other U.S. shops include locations in San Gabriel, CA; Los Angeles, CA; Irvine, CA; and Flushing, NY, with Brooklyn, NY, and San Jose, CA, coming soon.

  1. 2021

    Huzhou, China

    Mai Ji Milk (麦记牛奶), as Makkee is known in China, opens its first shop with a simple idea: traditional sweet soups made with fresh milk.

  2. Early 2025

    53 shops

    Makkee is still a small chain, with 53 shops across China.

  3. Late 2025

    Rowland Heights, California

    Makkee arrives in the U.S. in Southern California.

  4. Early 2026

    1,000+ shops

    In about a year, Makkee grows to roughly 1,000 shops across China.

  5. July 2026

    Mountain View, California

    A grand opening brings Makkee to the Bay Area.

  6. August 2026

    Sydney, Australia

    Makkee opens in Chinatown, Haymarket.

  7. September 2026

    Downtown Bellevue

    Our first shop in Washington opens on Main Street.

Made With Fresh Ingredients.

Every bowl starts with fresh ingredients: fresh milk, freshly cut fruit like mango and watermelon, and toppings prepared right in our shop.

Our taro balls, slow-cooked taro purée, and mango purée are all made in-house, so what you taste is the real thing.

What's in the Bowl.

Soft little pearls

Sago

Tiny, translucent pearls with a gentle bounce. They soak up all the good flavors.

Earthy & mellow

Taro

A root vegetable with a naturally creamy feel and a delicate, nutty sweetness.

Cool & herbal

Grass jelly

A softly set jelly with a clean herbal taste. Refreshing in a chilled dessert.

Bright & juicy

Pomelo

Citrus segments that pop with a gentle sweet-bitter sparkle next to mango.

Pillowy & chewy

Rice balls

Soft rice-flour bites that bring a satisfying, mochi-like chew to every spoonful.

Tender & creamy

Red bean

Tender, creamy red beans. You'll find them in many of our most-loved bowls.
